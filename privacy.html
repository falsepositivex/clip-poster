Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

clip-poster is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. This page explains what it does with data from Google (YouTube) and Instagram.

Data it accesses

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): it uploads videos to the owner's channel, sets their title, description, tags and privacy, and reads back the channel name and each uploaded video's processing and privacy status.
  • Instagram (content publishing): it publishes the owner's Reels to the owner's account and reads back each Reel's link.

It does not read comments, messages, subscribers, viewers, analytics or any other person's data.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens and a log of posted videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server run by clip-poster; there is none. The only data that leaves the computer is what is sent to YouTube and Instagram to publish the owner's videos.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google or Instagram is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

clip-poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Deleting the tool's folder on the owner's computer deletes all stored tokens and logs.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/falsepositivex/clip-poster.
