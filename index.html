clip-poster

clip-poster is a small personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and uploads short vertical videos that the owner made and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account, at times the owner chooses.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's finished clips to the owner's YouTube channel as Shorts, with the title, description and tags the owner approved.
  • Checks that each upload finished processing and is public.
  • Keeps a local log of what was posted and when.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, it has no sign-up, and it never acts on anyone else's account.

YouTube

clip-poster uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel. By using clip-poster you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
